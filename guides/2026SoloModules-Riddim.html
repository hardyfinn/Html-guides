Riddim

Dancehall riddim drum machine for VCV Rack. 30 HP, ten synthesised voices, 24 riddims, each with its own machine flavour.

Playing it

  • Send a 16th-note clock to CLOCK (one pulse per step). RESET goes back to step 1 of bar 1.
  • RIDDIM picks 1-24; the RIDDIM jack takes 0-10 V across the list. KIT 0 = the riddim's own machine; 1-8 forces CASIO, ROLAND 808, ROLAND PCM, LINN, SIMMONS, SAMPLER, WORKSTATION, LIVE. Try Dem Bow on the Simmons kit.
  • VAR: A is the main groove, B adds the fill-ins and extra hits, AUTO plays B on every fourth bar. A gate on VAR B forces B.
  • FILL (button or jack) plays one of four fills on the last beat of the bar. Right-click: auto fill every 2, 4 or 8 bars.
  • SWING pushes the odd 16ths late (up to 60% of a step). MUTATE adds ghost notes and drops soft hits, fresh each step. CRUSH lowers bit depth and sample rate on top of the kit's own, DRIVE adds saturation.
  • TUNE +-12 semitones, DECAY 0.5-2x, TONE brightens the noise in the snare, clap and hats, LEVEL sets the mix.
  • Each voice has a level knob (left) and its own output (right). Patching an output takes that voice out of the MIX L / MIX R pair, so you can send the kick to a compressor on its own.
  • The screen shows the riddim, year, producer, machine (red with ? where unconfirmed), and the 10 x 16 grid with the playhead. Dots at the left of the grid flash when a voice fires.

About the machines

  • Under Mi Sleng Teng (1985) was written on a preset of the Casio Casiotone MT-40 (Wikipedia). The Casio kit here is thin, 8-bit and crunchy to suit.
  • Rumours is the riddim behind J.C. Lodge's Telephone Love (Gussie Clarke, Music Works, 1988). A 2015 rebuild of it used an Oberheim DX drum machine, Yamaha DX100 and CS01; no source I found names the gear on the original, so the Linn/Oberheim kit is a guess.
  • For the Dave Kelly (Madhouse) and Lenky riddims no page I read names a drum machine, so they carry the workstation and sampler flavours as flavours only. If you know what they used, the label and kit are one line each in tools/make_patterns.py.
  • Real Rock and Stalag 17 were played by bands. They are here as reference grooves in the live kit.

Jacks

In: CLOCK (16ths), RESET, FILL (trigger), VAR B (gate), RIDDIM (0-10 V). Out: MIX L, MIX R, and ten voice outs (KICK, SNARE, RIM, CLAP, TOM, C HAT, O HAT, COW, PERC, SHAKE). A closed hat chokes the open hat. TOM steps carry three pitches (low, mid, high); PERC is a conga-like drum with ghost, normal and accent hits.
