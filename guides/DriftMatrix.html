The idea

DriftMatrix has 32 inputs and 32 outputs. Any input can reach any output through a "crosspoint", which makes 1024 of them. Instead of 1024 little knobs, you steer the randomness: how many crosspoints are active, whether they are on/off or at random levels, how much a reseed changes, and which two stored matrices you are mixing between.

Each crosspoint holds two hidden dice rolls. One decides whether it is active, the other decides its level. Because the dice are kept, turning CHANCE up adds crosspoints one at a time, and turning it down takes the same ones away again in reverse order. The matrix doesn't jump about.

Panel layout

  • Display (top): shows the mode, the chance, how many of the 1024 crosspoints are live, how many inputs and outputs are patched, which matrices A and B point to, and the mix amount.
  • Row 1, left to right: MODE switch, CHANCE knob + CV, RADICAL knob + CV, RESEED button + trigger input, SLEW knob + CV, GAIN switch.
  • Row 2, left to right: STORE button, eight slot buttons (1 to 8), A knob, B knob, MIX knob, SMOOTH/COIN switch, MIX CV, A/B CV.
  • Jacks (bottom): 32 inputs on the left (4 rows of 8) and 32 outputs on the right (4 rows of 8).

Controls

ControlWhat it does
MODEON/OFF: every active crosspoint is full level. 0-100%: each active crosspoint has its own random level (the default).
CHANCE (+CV)How likely each crosspoint is to be active (default 25%). Turning it up adds crosspoints one at a time, turning it down removes the same ones again. CV: +1 V = +10%.
RADICAL (+CV)How much RESEED changes (default 100%). All the way up = a brand-new matrix. Low = only a few crosspoints get new dice, so the routing mutates slowly. CV: +1 V = +10%.
RESEED button / trigger inputRolls new dice, as much as RADICAL says. The button lights briefly when it fires.
SLEW (+CV)How long every change takes, from 1 ms to 2 s (exponential knob). Keeps audio click-free; long settings make CV routings glide. CV: +1 V = +10%.
GAINAUTO (the default) keeps each output no louder than one full-level input, however many inputs land on it. SUM just adds them.
STORETap it (it blinks), then tap a slot to save the LIVE matrix there. While armed, every slot pulses white.
Slots 1 to 8After STORE: save here. On their own: load that snapshot back into LIVE (if the slot has something in it).
A, BPick two matrices each: L (LIVE) or slots 1 to 8. Defaults are A = LIVE, B = slot 1.
MIX (+CV)Fades from A (0%) to B (100%). CV: +1 V = +10%.
SMOOTH / COINSMOOTH crossfades every level (the default). COIN makes each crosspoint jump from A to B at its own point as MIX turns, so the routing changes piece by piece.
A/B CVMoves both A and B one place along per volt, through L, 1 ... 8 and round again. The CV is rounded to whole volts. Feed it a stepped CV to walk through your snapshots.
IN 1 to 32Signal inputs. Only patched inputs count.
OUT 1 to 32Signal outputs. Unpatched outputs cost no CPU.

Snapshots and mixing

A snapshot stores the matrix's dice, not its final levels. So CHANCE and MODE still shape a stored matrix live: a snapshot saved at 25% chance can be played back at 60%.

The slot lights tell you what is what: the slot used by A lights orange, the one used by B lights cyan (white if both), and stored slots glow dim. If A or B points at an empty slot, that side contributes nothing, and the display says (empty).

Right-click menu

  • Soft-clip outputs at about 10 V: handy with GAIN on SUM when things get hot.
  • Fill every empty slot with a fresh random matrix.
  • Clear all snapshots.

How to use it

  1. Patch some sources into a few inputs and take the outputs you want to a mixer or other modules.
  2. Turn CHANCE up slowly. More crosspoints appear one by one, so more inputs reach more outputs.
  3. Press RESEED for a different routing. With RADICAL at 100% it is brand new; turn RADICAL down to about 5% for a small mutation of what you have.
  4. When you hear a routing you like, press STORE then a slot.
  5. Store a few, set A and B to two of them, and turn MIX (or feed it a CV) to drift between them.

Patch ideas

  • Drifting mixer: eight oscillators or loops into inputs 1 to 8, outputs 1 to 4 to a stereo mixer. Slow LFO into MIX, A and B set to two stored snapshots, COIN mode.
  • Mutating routing: a clock into the RESEED input with RADICAL near 10%. Every clock a few connections change, so the patch evolves slowly instead of jumping.
  • CV router: run envelopes and LFOs through it, with long SLEW. Modulation glides from one destination to another.
  • Snapshot walker: a sequencer or stepped random into A/B CV, with MIX at 0%, to step through all your stored matrices.

Notes

  • The matrix, snapshots and soft-clip setting are saved with the patch.
  • Randomise (the Rack menu) reseeds the matrix completely. MODE, GAIN, A, B, SMOOTH/COIN and SLEW are excluded from randomise.
