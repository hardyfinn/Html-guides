Divskip

VCV Rack 2 module by Haer, 32 HP (repo name: Divskeep): four channels that split a clock between two outputs

Each channel takes a clock (or any triggers) and splits it between two outputs, A (the hits that play) and B (the hits that are skipped). Inspired by the DivSkip Eurorack module by DivKid and Making Sound Machines; this is an independent VCV version with its own panel, drum patterns and extra modes.

The idea

Every mode answers one question for each incoming clock: does it go to A, to B, to both, or to neither? Patch a 16th-note clock into channel 1. Empty INs on channels 2 to 4 use the channel above, so one clock drives all four.

Each channel

PartWhat it does
INClock or triggers. An empty IN uses the channel above.
MODE (jack)Moves the mode on: each +1 V is one mode further, wrapping round. Good for a sequencer.
CV + AMOUNTThe mode's main setting. CV is ±5 V and is added to the knob (the knob spans 0 to 5 V). The light ring shows the steps: A in the mode's colour, B cyan, playhead white.
CV + SHAPEThe mode's second setting. Each mode remembers its own SHAPE position; double-click resets it to that mode's default. Dimmed in modes that don't use it.
DisplayMode, AMOUNT and SHAPE in words. Click = next mode, Shift-click = previous, Ctrl/Cmd-click = new seed for this channel, right-click = mode list and Chain.
SEED (jack)A trigger gives this channel a new seed (COIN, TURING, RANDOM, STICKY).
MUTESilences A and B. The channel keeps counting.
A / BThe two outputs, 10 V.

The ten modes

#ModeAMOUNTSHAPE (default)
1COINEach hit goes to A or B at random. Left = always A, noon = 50/50, right = always B.Loop: off, 4, 8, 16, 32 steps (16). Looped, the same tosses repeat; AMOUNT still tips them.
2DIVIDEClock divider on A and B, lengths add up to 16. Noon = /8 and /8, left = /1 and /15, right = /15 and /1.Rotate both by 0 to 15 steps (0).
3TURINGA looping random pattern (set steps to A, unset to B). Left half: loop length 1 to 16, locked. Right half: 16 steps, increasingly likely to change. Full left = always A.Mask: a second random loop of 0 (off) to 8 steps. Where it is off, A and B are both silent (0).
4SPLITEuclid split: A plays 3 hits over its part of 16, B plays 3 over the rest. Noon = 3:3:2 on both.Not used.
5EUCLIDLoop length, 1 to 16 steps. A plays the hits, B the gaps.Number of hits (3).
6RAMPEach clock becomes a run of hits (alternating A, B) that speed up (left), speed up then slow (noon) or slow down (right). In gate mode: a gate on A, then one on B, with the length set exponentially.Number of hits, 1 to 16 (16).
7RATCHETEach clock becomes a burst: A gets n, B gets 16 − n evenly spaced hits. In gate mode: A/B gate lengths in fine, even steps.Divide the clock first: off, /2, /4, /8 (off).
8PATTERN24 four-bar drum patterns per channel, from a 16th-note clock. Ch 1 kick/snare, ch 2 closed/open hat, ch 3 low/high perc, ch 4 ride/accents.Not used.
9RANDOMHow many of the steps hit; the hits sit in random places. Turning it adds or removes one hit at a time.Number of steps, 1 to 32 (16).
10STICKYSticky coin: how likely each hit is to go to the same output as the last. Left = swaps every hit, noon = coin toss, right = long runs.Loop: off, 4, 8, 16, 32 steps (off).

RAMP and RATCHET fit their hits into the time between your last two clocks.

Chain

Right-click a channel's display (or the module) and tick Chain to channel above. Chained channels play one after another as one long loop: channel 1 plays its loop once, then channel 2, and so on, then back to channel 1. Each channel keeps its own mode and setting, so a chain can be 16 RANDOM steps then a TURING loop of 7. A loop is 16 clocks, or the TURING / EUCLID / RANDOM length, or the COIN / STICKY loop length when one is set.

  • The whole chain runs on the top channel's clock.
  • The top channel's A and B send the whole chain. Each other channel's A and B send only its own turn.
  • A display linked by a bar on its left is chained; it dims while waiting its turn.

Global controls

  • OUTPUTS: trig sends 5 ms triggers. gate holds each output high until the next clock, so two A steps in a row give one long gate.
  • MUTE SET: two sets of mutes, M1 (cyan) and M2 (orange). Flip the switch to jump between two mute scenes.
  • NEW SEED button and jack: a new seed for every channel in COIN, TURING, RANDOM or STICKY. Seeds are saved with the patch.
  • Gate gap (right-click the module): in gate mode, gates end 2 ms before the next clock, so two A steps in a row are two gates.
  • RESET: all channels back to step 1. A channel also returns to step 1 after 3 seconds without a clock.

Patch ideas

  • Kick and hat from one clock: channel 1 in DIVIDE, A to a kick and B to a hat. Turn AMOUNT to change the division of each.
  • Drum kit in four channels: set all four channels to PATTERN with a 16th clock and use the kit layout (kick/snare, hats, perc, ride/accents). Patch RESET so bar 1 lands where you want.
  • Evolving percussion: TURING just right of noon on one channel, then turn it left of noon to lock a pattern you like.
  • Fills: a slow clock into RAMP or RATCHET for rolls, switched in with a mute set on the last bar of a phrase.
  • Longer phrases: chain a DIVIDE channel, a RANDOM channel and a TURING channel and play the whole chain from the top channel's A and B.
  • Mode changes in time: a sequencer into a MODE jack to jump to another mode on chosen bars.
