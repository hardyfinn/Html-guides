Boohm for VCV Rack

A stereo kick drum voice with four models (ANLG, FM-2, MEMB, GRIT), modelled on the control layout of the Ohmforce Bohm. By Haer.

What it does

A stereo kick drum voice with four sound models, built as a companion to the Ohmforce Bohm Eurorack kick: the controls follow the Bohm's panel (HIT, VELOCITY, LENGTH, SUSTAIN, ATTACK, PITCH, CURVE, COLOR, TRS DECAY, TRS TONE, FX, STEREO) so ideas move between hardware and Rack. The sound engines, DSP and panel are original. Boohm is not affiliated with or endorsed by Ohm Force.

Every main knob has a CV jack. The CV adds to the knob, and 10 V sweeps the whole range.

Controls

ControlWhat it does
HIT (button + jack)Plays the kick 1 ms after the trigger so pitch, velocity and model CVs sent with it are caught. A short trigger plays the whole kick. If the gate stays high, the body sustains until release (can be switched off in the right-click menu).
PITCHBase note, C1 to C2. The ATV below it scales the PITCH CV; with ±5 V and the ATV fully up, the CV moves pitch ±1 octave.
CURVEShape of the pitch drop. Left is the fast 808-style drop; right holds the pitch up briefly then drops, like a 909.
LENGTHTotal length, 40 ms to 2.5 s.
ATTACKClick level plus a punch boost in the first ~20 ms. FM depth on FM-2, beater level on MEMB.
SUSTAINBody level (room-mic level on MEMB). The ATV below it scales its CV.
COLORBody timbre, depends on the model.
TRS DECAY / TRS TONEClick length (1–150 ms) and click tone (dark to bright).
VELOCITYOutput level. The VEL jack sets each hit's level when played (10 V = full).
FX / FX TYPEDrive amount and its type: TUBE, BASS (lows only), SOFT, HARD, FOLD, CRUSH.
STEREOWidth of the click, harmonics and room. The sub stays mono.
RANDOM (button + jack)Picks a new sound. Keeps the model and velocity unless RANDOM also changes the model is ticked.
ENV / ENV INVThe body envelope, 0–10 V, and its inverse (10 V falling to 0). Put ENV INV on a VCA for sidechain-style ducking.
L / RAudio out. If only L is patched it carries a mono mix.

Other jacks: MODEL CV (2.5 V per model), FX TYPE CV (about 1.7 V per type). Lights show the current model and FX type, plus HIT and RANDOM activity; a model button and an FX type button step through them.

Models

  • ANLG: analogue-style body. COLOR morphs sine → triangle → warm saturated → squarish. Noise-and-tick click.
  • FM-2: two-operator FM. ATTACK = FM depth, TRS DECAY = FM decay (10–100 ms), TRS TONE picks one of 8 modulator waves, COLOR = carrier (sine → triangle → square). Set the ratio (0.5–15, default 2) in the right-click menu.
  • MEMB: a struck drum head (8 modes) played by a beater, heard through a small room. PITCH = head size, CURVE = pitch bend after the hit, LENGTH = head damping, ATTACK = beater click, TRS TONE = beater hardness, COLOR = shell overtones, SUSTAIN = room mic level, TRS DECAY = room size, STEREO = room width.
  • GRIT: eight digital single-cycle tables scanned by COLOR, with a metallic FM tick on top of the click.

Right-click menu

  • Model and FX type pickers
  • FM-2 ratio
  • PITCH CV tracks 1 V/oct (the PITCH knob becomes an offset above C1 and the ATV is ignored)
  • Held HIT sustains the kick (on by default)
  • RANDOM also changes the model

Patch ideas

Ducking bass

Patch ENV INV into a VCA (or level CV) on your bass or pad so it ducks on every kick.

Pitched kick line

Turn on PITCH CV tracking and send a sequencer's pitch to PITCH; trigger HIT from the same sequencer for a tuned 808-style bass.

Evolving kicks

Send a clock to RANDOM with the model change ticked: each hit may be a different sound. Leave it off to keep one model and vary only the settings.

Accented kick

Send a stepped or random voltage to the VEL jack; it is read at each hit (10 V = full level).

Stereo drum bus

Patch L and R into a stereo mixer channel; raise STEREO for a wider click and room (the sub stays mono).
