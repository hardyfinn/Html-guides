Kleener

16-bit loop sequencer for VCV Rack, by Haer

Kleener is a 16-bit loop sequencer for VCV Rack, inspired by the Klee sequencer (original design and code). It is one 30 HP module by Haer, plugin version 2.0.0, for Rack 2.

The idea

Sixteen on/off bits sit in a loop that turns one step on every clock. Eight pitch-weight sliders look at the first eight bits of the loop. Every slider whose bit is on adds its amount to the pitch. As the loop turns, a different set of bits sits under the sliders, so one short pattern of on/off switches becomes a whole melody.

Each step also has a bus switch (1, 2 or 3) that sends its gate to one of three gate outputs, so one pattern can play a kick, a hat and a stab at once.

Controls

ControlWhat it does
BIT 1-16 (white buttons)The pattern. Lit = on. By default a press changes the loop straight away.
Amber light under each bitWhat is in the loop right now (mutation can make it differ from the buttons).
White dot under each bus switchThe playhead.
BUS switch per stepDown = gate on BUS 1, middle = BUS 2, up = BUS 3.
+1 ... +8 slidersPitch weight of bit 1-8 of the loop window. Full slider = 12 semitones at RANGE 100%. Tick marks are semitones.
LENGTH + CVLoop length, 2-16 steps. +1 V = +1.5 steps.
MUTATE + CVChance that a bit flips as it leaves the playhead. Fully left = locked loop. A little = slowly evolving. Fully right = the loop inverts every pass. +1 V = +10%.
RANGE + CVScales all sliders together, 0-200%. Turn down for small intervals, up for leaps. +1 V = +10%.
GLIDESlide between notes on both pitch outputs (up to about half a second).
SCALEQuantiser, root C: OFF, CHROMatic, MINOR, DORIAN, PHRYGian, minor PENTAtonic. The light shows which one is on.
LOAD (button and jack)Copies the bit buttons into the loop, undoing any mutation.
CLOCKEach rising edge moves the loop one step.
RESETBack to step 1 on the next clock, and loads the buttons.
FLIPA trigger here makes the next bit flip for sure.
PITCH AV/OCT from bits 1-8 of the loop window.
PITCH BV/OCT from bits 9-16 (the far half of the loop): a related second line.
BUS GATES 1, 2, 3Gate while the clock is high, on steps whose bit is on and whose bus switch points there.
ANYGate on every step whose bit is on.

Right-click menu

  • Pitch B reads: the next 8 bits (default) or the switched-off bits, which makes B the "opposite" of A.
  • Tie gates: hold a bus gate across neighbouring steps on the same bus, for legato lines.
  • Switches change the loop straight away: turn off for the classic way, where the buttons are only read on LOAD/RESET. Then you can set up the next pattern while the old one plays and drop it in with LOAD.
  • RESET also loads the switches.
  • Copy the loop onto the switches: keep a mutation you like.
  • Random switches, Random bus switches.

Patch ideas

1. Detroit bassline with a pulse

Clock from your master clock at 16ths. PITCH A to a bass oscillator V/OCT, BUS 1 to its envelope. Set SCALE MINOR, RANGE about 40%, LENGTH 16, MUTATE fully left. Put bus switches to 1 on the steps you want the bass to speak. Turn LENGTH to 12 or 7 against a 16-step drum loop for a rolling, never-quite-repeating feel.

2. Three drums from one pattern

BUS 1 to a kick, BUS 2 to a snare or clap, BUS 3 to a hat, ANY to an accent or a filter envelope. Every step whose bit is on plays one of the three. Nudge MUTATE to about 5-10% and the groove slowly rewrites itself while keeping its shape. Press LOAD to snap back to the original.

3. Self-mutation

Patch a slow random or a Marbles-style trigger stream into FLIP, and an LFO into MUTATE CV. Put PITCH A through GLIDE into an FM voice and PITCH B into a second voice an octave up. With "Switches change the loop straight away" off, set a new pattern on the buttons and fire LOAD from a Long Form or bar counter every 8 bars, so the piece moves between sections on its own.

4. Melody and countermelody

Set Pitch B to "switched-off bits". A plays whenever B rests and the other way round. Send A to a lead and B to a pad, both clocked by ANY.

Starting points

  • Sliders: try 3, 7, 2, 5, 12, 10, 5, 0 semitones (the defaults). Sums land on chord tones when SCALE is MINOR.
  • Want octaves only: set one slider to full and the rest to 0, RANGE 100%.
  • For acid lines: RANGE 60-80%, GLIDE about 20%, Tie gates on.
