Overview

The plugin has two modules. Seuperclidean plays six Euclidean rhythms (Toussaint / Bjorklund) at once, one per row. On top of the classic algorithm it adds a Bias warp, self-mutation, density and fills, swing and humanise, and logic outputs that combine pairs of channels. Seuperclidean 4 is the same idea with four voices in columns.

Each channel has three outputs: GATE (the rhythm), OFF (every step the rhythm doesn't play) and ACC (only the pattern's downbeat hit).

Per-channel controls (rows 1 to 6)

ControlWhat it does
LENPattern length, 1 to 32 steps.
FILLNumber of hits, spread as evenly as possible. E(3,8) = x..x..x.
ROTRotates the whole pattern later by whole steps.
BIASWarps the bar. 0 = classic Euclidean. Negative crowds the hits early, leaving a gap at the end. Positive pushes them late, rolling into the next downbeat. The first hit always stays on the pattern's downbeat and the number of hits never changes.
SWGSwing, 50% (straight) to 75%. Delays every second 16th.
HUMHumanise: each hit lands randomly 0 to 30 ms late.
MUTChance that the pattern mutates at the end of each cycle.
FILL / ROT / BIAS CVFill: 10 V adds a full length of hits. Rot: 10 V = one full turn. Bias: ±5 V sweeps the whole range.
GATE outThe rhythm.
OFF outThe gaps: every step the rhythm doesn't play.
ACC outOnly the pattern's downbeat hit.

Mutation

When a channel's pattern comes round to its first step, it may (MUT × MUTATE chance) change one thing: rotation by a step, bias by 25%, one hit more or less, or one step flipped on or off. About a third of mutations move a change back towards the knob settings, so patterns wander around home rather than drifting off for good.

In the display, orange is a normal hit, cyan is a hit that mutation added, and dark brown is a hit that mutation removed.

ControlWhat it does
MUTATE + CVScales every channel's MUT (default 100%).
LOCK button / gateFreezes the current mutated patterns.
HOMEClears all mutation, back to the knob settings.

Mutated patterns are saved with the patch.

Density and fills

ControlWhat it does
DENSITY + CVAdds hits to (right) or removes them from (left) every channel at once, as a share of each channel's length. ±5 V sweeps the whole range.
FILL button / gateWhile held, every channel fills up its empty steps by AMT and leans its Bias late, so it rolls into the next bar.
AMTHow strong the fill is.

Logic outputs

Each logic output compares two channels step by step: 1+2, 3+4 and 5+6. Press the button to choose the mode:

  • AND: both channels hit
  • OR: either channel hits
  • XOR: one hits but not both
  • A NOT B: the first channel hits and the second doesn't

The logic outputs use the first channel's swing.

Clock and reset

CLOCK takes 16th notes. RESET sends every channel back to step 1 on the next clock. Right-click the module to choose trigger (5 ms) or gate (half a step) outputs.

Seuperclidean 4

A 28 HP four-voice version in the same plugin. Each voice has its own column under a step ring, with the same controls as a row on the 6-channel module. The ring's centre shows hits/length.

  • Four logic outputs: 1+2, 3+4, and the cross pairs 1+3 and 2+4. Click the mode readout above an output to change it (AND, OR, XOR, ONLY n = the first voice hits and the second doesn't).
  • Bottom strip: CLOCK, RESET, FILL gate, DENS (density) and MUT (mutate) trimpots, and LOCK.
  • Right-click menu: fill amount, Home and the output shape.

Patch ideas

These follow from the controls above:

  • Kick, snare, hat: clock the module with 16ths, set LEN 16 on three channels with FILL 4, 2 and 9, and send each GATE to a drum voice. Move BIAS slowly to push hits early or late without changing how many there are.
  • Hits and gaps: use a channel's OFF output for a second voice (a hat, say) that only plays where the first voice rests.
  • Self-evolving groove: raise MUTATE and let patterns wander. When you like what you hear, press LOCK; press HOME to return to the knob settings.
  • Build-ups: send a button or gate to FILL at the end of a phrase, or a slow envelope to DENSITY.
  • Accents: send ACC to a VCA or accent input so only each pattern's downbeat is emphasised.
  • Logic layers: use XOR on a pair of channels to get a third rhythm made of the places where they disagree.

Building

GitHub Actions builds the Apple Silicon version on every push. Locally, download the Rack SDK and run make dist RACK_DIR=/path/to/Rack-SDK. The panel is drawn by res/make_panel.py (needs pip install fonttools). The rhythm engine (src/EuclidEngine.hpp) has no Rack dependencies, and test/engine_test.cpp checks it offline.
