Symbolic for VCV Rack

Symbolic is an acoustic cymbal (and hi-hat) for VCV Rack 2, by Haer. It is not a sample and not an 808-style square-wave cymbal: it models a bronze plate ringing at 228 frequencies, how the stick hits it, and the shimmer that blooms after a hard hit. It includes a hi-hat pedal and stereo output.

How it works

A cymbal rings at hundreds of unrelated pitches at once. Symbolic has 228 of them: a few low ones for body and slow wobble, a crowd of high ones for sizzle, and 12 bell tones for the dome. The stick decides which ring and how bright the hit is, by where you hit (bell, bow, edge) and what with (hard tip or soft mallet).

When a cymbal is hit hard, the big low vibrations pass their energy up to the high tones over the first moments. That is the hiss that blooms just after a crash. Soft hits barely do it, so a gently played ride pings and a hard one washes. Symbolic does the same. Hits overlap like on a real cymbal: a new hit adds to the ringing instead of restarting it.

Quick start

  1. Patch a gate into TRIG and OUT L (and OUT R for stereo) to your mixer. Press HIT to audition.
  2. Right-click the module for starting points: Ride, 20", Crash, 16" and Hi-hat, 14".
  3. Patch a velocity or accent CV (0–10 V) into VEL. Harder hits are brighter and wash more. Unpatched, every trigger is 7.5 V.

The panel

Plate

ControlWhat it does
SIZE + CVDiameter, 8" to 24" (default 18"). Bigger is lower, darker and rings longer. CV: ±5 V covers the full range.
WEIGHTThin to heavy. Thin cymbals are low, quick and washy; heavy ones are higher, ring longer and ping more.
BELLSize of the dome. Matters most when POSITION is near the bell.
WOBBLEThe slow beating of the low tones, like an imperfectly round cymbal. 0 is a perfect disc.
TRASHTurns it into a china: pitch bends down as the hit dies, beating gets fast and the wash gets gritty.
CASTSixteen individual cymbals with the same settings, each with its own pattern of tones. Shown as "Cymbal no. N".

Strike and ring

ControlWhat it does
POSITION + CVWhere the stick lands: bell, bow (middle) or edge. The hover text names the zone. CV is read at each hit (±5 V = full range).
STICK + CVHard tip (left) to soft mallet (right): brighter and sharper to darker and rounder. CV is read at each hit.
DECAY + CVHow long it rings, from a short tick to over 30 seconds.
DAMPHow fast high tones die compared with low ones. Left: bright sizzling tail. Right: dark tail.
SHIMMER + CVHow much energy moves up into the high wash after hard hits. Low is pingy; high is an explosive wash.
BUILDHow slowly the wash blooms: fast is an immediate hiss, slow lets the hit come first.

Hi-hat and output

ControlWhat it does
TUNEShifts every tone up or down, ±24 semitones. V/OCT input adds to it.
PEDAL + CVHi-hat pedal. Open (left) rings freely; half-way the plates rattle (sizzle); closed (right) is a tight "tsst". CV (0–10 V) adds to the knob.
CHICKLevel of the foot chick when the pedal closes past 80%.
WIDTHStereo width: the two outputs are like two microphones at different spots.
LEVELOutput level.
HITButton that plays a hit at 7.5 V velocity; its light shows the cymbal ringing.

Jacks

ControlWhat it does
V/OCTTunes every tone, 1 V per octave.
TRIGTrigger input; plays a hit.
VELVelocity, 0–10 V, read at each trigger. Unpatched = 7.5 V.
CHOKEA hand grabbing the cymbal. A trigger grabs it for 150 ms; a held gate keeps it held.
SIZE, POSITION, STICK, DECAY, SHIMMER, PEDALCV inputs, as described above.
OUT L / OUT RStereo output. With OUT R unpatched, OUT L carries a mono mix.

Recipes

  • Hi-hat, one module. Choose the Hi-hat start, then turn PEDAL fully left (open). Hat triggers to TRIG, a closed gate to the PEDAL jack (10 V = closed): low gate is open, high gives the foot chick and cuts the ring. Velocity into VEL for accents. Or a slow 0–7 V LFO into PEDAL for a foot that opens and closes over bars.
  • Ride pattern. Ride start, triggers with velocity high on beats and lower on skips, STICK low. A slow random voltage into POSITION CV makes the stick wander on the bow.
  • Crash with hand choke. Crash start, trigger on bar 1, and a gate into CHOKE to grab it before a drop.
  • Self-mutating cymbal. A fast clock into TRIG, slow unrelated random voltages into POSITION, STICK and SHIMMER CVs and a random velocity into VEL. Because hits overlap, the wash keeps reshaping itself.
  • Tuned metal. WOBBLE 0, DECAY high, DAMP right, SHIMMER low, POSITION near the bell. Sequence V/OCT for inharmonic bell tones, and change CAST for a different instrument.

Notes

  • Different from an 808-style cymbal, which copies the TR-808 circuit; Symbolic models a real plate.
