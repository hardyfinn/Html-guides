Overview

Two VCV Rack 2 modules by Haer for playing a whole track instead of a loop, joined by one poly cable (LINK).

  • Long Form (52 HP) is an arrangement conductor. You draw a song form on an 8 × 8 grid (sections down, lanes across) and it tells everything else when to come in, drop out and build. STEER picks the energy you want next, and DRIFT lets the form rewrite itself a little after each section.
  • Mixxa (58 HP) is an 8-channel performance mixer. Every mute, solo, scene change and random roll lands on the next beat. It also has kick ducking and a one-knob glue compressor.

First patch

  1. Clock (16th notes) into Long Form CLOCK, and the clock's reset into RESET.
  2. Long Form LINK → Mixxa LINK.
  3. Your parts into Mixxa: kick on 1, then bass, hats, clap, perc, chords, lead and texture on 2 to 8.
  4. Mixxa OUT L / R to your audio interface.
  5. Press play. The default form (Intro 16, Groove 32, Build 8, Peak 32, Break 16, Drop 8, Peak 2 32, Outro 16 bars) runs about 6 minutes at 130 BPM, bringing parts in and out.

Starting values: DUCK about 60% on bass and chords, RELEASE 160 ms, GLUE at 9 o'clock.

Outputs: L1 to L8 lane gates; ACT (poly 8, 10 V = play; channels 1 to 6 match Francois Markovian's A to F); ENERGY; RAMP (0 to 10 V over each section); FILL; CHANGE; BAR; PHRASE (every 4 bars; menu 2 to 16); END; LINK.

Drift-edited cells show a small amber dot, and the cell flashes as the edit happens. The display shows the form as blocks (width = length, brightness = energy), the playhead, the cued section in cyan, and the last drift edit. Drift is seeded and saved with the patch, so a patch replays the same drift; right-click for a new seed.

Right-click: clock rate (16ths, 24 PPQN, 8ths, quarters), cue grid, phrase length, loop or stop at the end, section and lane names, new drift seed, clear the grid.

Mixxa

Next-beat mutes and solos

Press MUTE and it blinks orange: the change is armed. On the next beat it happens. Press again before the beat to cancel. SOLO works the same way. Each mute fades over 4 ms (menu: 2 to 20 ms). Right-click Mute timing for next beat (default), next 16th or immediate. With no clock at all, mutes act at once.

MUTE colours: blinking orange = armed, red = muted, amber = you have overridden Long Form (it hands the channel back at the next section change, unless Manual mutes stay is on).

Scenes A to D

Hold a scene button for 1 second to store the current mutes. Tap to recall on the next beat. SCENE CV: 0 to 4 V picks A to D.

Random mutes

DICE or a trigger at TRIG mutes COUNT random channels on the next beat and unmutes the rest. Only channels with an input count, and channels Long Form has muted are left alone. COUNT CV adds -5 to +5 V → 0 to 8. BACK returns to the mutes from before the last roll. The KICK IN/OUT switch decides whether channel 1 can be picked (default IN); right-click to leave out any of channels 2 to 8. "Always a new set" (on by default) re-rolls if a roll would repeat the current set.

Kick ducking

KICK takes a trigger or the kick's audio; unpatched, channel 1's audio is used. Each channel's DUCK sets the depth (channel 1 never ducks). RELEASE: 40 to 400 ms. SHAPE: gate-like, pump (noon), smooth swell.

Master

GLUE is a 2:1 bus compressor (10 ms attack, 150 ms release, soft knee) with up to about 4 dB of gain reduction and automatic make-up. MASTER is the output level (0 dB at about 1 o'clock, +6 dB at full). STEMS is poly 16: channels 1 to 8 left, 9 to 16 right, post-fader and post-mute.

Inputs

CLOCK (16ths; ignored when LINK is patched), ACT (poly 8: 10 V = play, quantised to the beat), LINK.

Patch ideas

  • Hands-off arrangement: the first patch above. Long Form's lane gates drive Mixxa's mutes through LINK, so parts come and go on their own.
  • Let the form evolve: raise DRIFT a little and lower FOLLOW below 100%. Use LOCK to freeze it when you like it, RESTORE to go back to your drawn form.
  • Drive other modules: ENERGY or RAMP into filter cutoffs or reverb sends for builds; FILL and CHANGE for fills and section transitions; ACT into Francois Markovian.
  • Drop beats by hand: mute and solo on Mixxa land on the beat. Store breakdown mutes in a scene and recall it with one tap, or use random mutes with BACK to undo.
  • Sections for other sequencers: a Kleener LOAD fired from Long Form's CHANGE or PHRASE output moves a pattern on at each section.
