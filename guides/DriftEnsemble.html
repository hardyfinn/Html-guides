Drift Ensemble

VCV Rack 2 module by Haer: one steady 16th-note clock in, six human-feel players out

Each of six "players" drifts against the clock the way human musicians do, following the 1/f timing model from Hennig et al., "The Nature and Perception of Fluctuations in Human Musical Rhythms", PLoS ONE 6(10): e26457 (2011).

The idea

Patch a steady 16th-note clock (from Live, CV Tools, a clock module) into Clock. Each player's output is that clock with human-style timing: slightly early or late, with slow wandering. Players can hit before the clock, because the module predicts the next tick from the measured tempo, so there is no added latency.

Controls

ControlWhat it does
Clock (in)Steady 16th-note clock.
Reset (in)Pulls every player's drift back to zero (it then grows again). Marks the downbeat for Swing, Anchor and Push.
DisplayEach player's last hit: left of centre = early, right = late (edge = 50 ms). Player 1 shows pink in Leader mode.
Spread (per player)How far the player wanders: 0 to 50 ms, noon = 15 ms, default 0.
Lean (per player)Fixed push or lay-back, −35 ms (early) to +35 ms (late). Drift wobbles around it.
Swing (per player)50 % (straight) to 75 %, added to the master Swing; the total is capped at 75 %.
Out (per player)The player's clock: a trigger, or a half-step gate (right-click menu).
Together + CVBand mode: 0 % = players drift independently, 100 % = the band drifts as one. Leader mode: how closely players 2 to 6 follow player 1.
Leader (button)Switches Together to Leader mode; player 1 leads (its light turns pink). Followers react to the leader's previous hit by default.
Spread ± + CVMaster offset added to every player's Spread, −20 to +20 ms. Each player stays within 0 to 50 ms.
Seed0 = new drift every time. 1 to 32 = Reset (or the first clock) replays exactly the same drift.
Feel + CVColour of the drift for the whole band (beta 0 to 2): jitter, nervous, human (noon), loose, wander.
Anchor0 to 100 %: how much drift and Lean shrink on each bar's first 16th, so the band lands together on the "one".
Swing + CVMaster swing: delays every second 16th, 50 % (straight) to 75 %.
Push0 to 30 ms: everyone rushes gradually through the last bar of each phrase.
Energy + CVScales all drift, 0 to 100 %. Lean, Swing and Push are unaffected.
Freeze (button + gate)Holds every player's current offset until released.

CV inputs: 10 V sweeps the whole knob range.

Right-click menu

  • Greyscale panel
  • Output shape (trigger or half-step gate)
  • Leader reacts to previous hit
  • Bar length: 12, 16, 24 or 32 steps (default 16)
  • Phrase length for Push: 2, 4, 8 or 16 bars (default 8)
  • Loosen up after Reset: off, or ramp Energy up over 8, 16, 32 or 64 bars

Sequencer-safe timing

Every player outputs exactly one pulse per input clock, in order, through clock stops, restarts and Reset. A player running early may play its next pulse just before the clock stops; it then skips that pulse when the clock restarts, so counts always line up. You can clock sequencers and drum voices from the player outputs without them losing their place.

Patch ideas

  • Loose drum kit: one player per drum voice (kick, snare, hats...) from a stiff 16th clock. Raise Spread a little per player and set Together around the middle.
  • Tight band: Together at 100 % and Anchor up, so the band drifts as one and lands together on the downbeat.
  • Drummer leading: press Leader, put the kick on player 1 and raise Together so the others follow it.
  • Behind the beat: positive Lean on the snare player, a little negative Lean on the hats.
  • Repeatable take: set Seed to 1 to 32 so Reset replays the same drift every pass. Seed 0 gives a new feel each time.
  • Build the feel in: use "Loosen up after Reset" in the menu so Energy ramps up over several bars after each Reset.
