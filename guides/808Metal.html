What it does

The TR-808's cymbal and hi-hat circuit, built stage by stage from the schematic, with extra controls so you can push it well past the 808. Turn every knob to its default (right-click, Reset to the pure model) and you hear the circuit as built.

6 square oscillators → 2 bandpass filters (about 3.4 kHz and 7.1 kHz) → 3 swing VCAs with their own decays and a soft clip → 3 highpass filters → OUT

The 808 makes its metal sounds from six square-wave oscillators at clashing pitches (205, 304, 370, 523, 540 and 800 Hz). The bandpass filters pick out the bright, clangy part, the VCAs shape it and the highpass filters remove the low end. The engine runs at twice the host sample rate so the high band and the resonant 10 kHz highpass stay true to the model.

Credit. The circuit model is by mjsyts, from the post "TR808 Cymbal Hardware Accurate Modeling" on scsynth.org (SuperCollider). This plugin is a new C++ implementation; only the published circuit values are used, none of the post's code is copied.

Quick start

Patch a clock or sequencer gate into TRIG and OUT to your mixer. Press HIT to audition. Flick MODE to choose the sound: CH closed hat, OH open hat, CY cymbal. MODE sets the range of the DECAY knob.

Controls

Rows run top to bottom: oscillators, filters, envelope, CV inputs, then trigger inputs and output.

Oscillators

ControlWhat it does
TUNE (+ V/OCT)Moves all six oscillators together, ratios kept (±2 octaves).
TRIM A / TRIM BThe 808's two trimmer-tuned oscillators (800 Hz and 540 Hz), over the hardware's ranges.
SPREADLeft squeezes the gaps between the six pitches, right stretches them.
WIDTHPulse width, 47.98 % as built.
DRIFTSlow random detune per oscillator, up to about ±40 cents.

Filters

ControlWhat it does
COLOUR (+ CV)Shifts both bandpass filters up or down (1 V/oct on the CV).
RESCentre is as built. Right gives narrower, ringing bandpass filters; left gives wider ones.
BANDBalance of the 3.4 kHz and 7.1 kHz filters.
CUT (+ CV)Moves the highpass filters (1 V/oct on the CV).
EXT INAny audio here replaces the six oscillators, so the filters and VCAs work on your sound.
OSC OUTThe six oscillators, raw.

Envelope and output

ControlWhat it does
DECAY (+ CV)Decay time, range set by MODE. ±5 V on the CV covers the full range.
MODECH 20–150 ms, OH 0.09–0.6 s, CY 0.35–1.2 s (the 808's ranges).
TONE (+ CV)Balance of the low path (long decay) against the two higher paths (shorter decays). ±5 V = full range.
DRIVE (+ CV)Pushes the VCAs into their soft clip. CV is 0–10 V.
LEVELOutput level.
HITManual trigger button; it lights with the envelope.
MAIN OUTThe audio output.

Gates

ControlWhat it does
TRIGTrigger input.
ACCENTRead at each trigger. 0 V plays normally; 10 V is +3 dB and drives the clip harder.
CHOKEA trigger here cuts the sound in 3 ms, for example from a closed hat.

Right-click menu: One envelope for all three paths (the original post's behaviour) and Reset to the pure model.

Patch ideas

808 hat pair with choke

Use two modules. Module 1 on CH, module 2 on OH. Send the closed-hat gate to module 1 TRIG and to module 2 CHOKE, and the open-hat gate to module 2 TRIG. Every closed hat now cuts the open hat.

Detroit ride

MODE CY, DECAY low, TONE a little right, a touch of DRIVE. Trigger off-beat 8ths and send an accent gate to ACCENT.

Tuned metal percussion

Turn SPREAD left and RES right so the oscillators pull towards one pitch and the filters ring. Send a sequencer pitch to V/OCT for pitched bells.

Self-mutating hats

Fast clock into TRIG; slow random voltages into DECAY, TONE and COLOUR CV, and a probability gate into ACCENT.

808 filters on anything

Patch a loop, noise or a synth into EXT IN, trigger on 16ths, MODE CH. The filters and VCAs chop it into metallic hats.

Notes

Peaks reach about 7 V at default settings and the output never passes ±12 V. OSC OUT is about ±4.5 V. Version 2.1.0, licence GPL-3.0-or-later.
