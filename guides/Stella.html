Stella for VCV Rack

Stella is a four-voice sample player by Haer. It lays a folder of one-shots out on a 2D map according to how they sound, so you can pick samples by moving a point around the map, with CV, by hand, or by letting it drift.

How it works

When you load a folder, Stella listens to every WAV and AIFF in it (up to 1000 files, subfolders included, each trimmed to 2 seconds) and places each one as a dot on the map. Each of the four voices has a point on the map. A trigger plays the sample nearest that point. With probability CHANCE, it plays another sample inside the voice's SPREAD ring instead, with closer samples more likely.

Dots are coloured from warm (heavy, low) through white to cool (bright, noisy).

Loading samples

  • Press LOAD to choose a folder, or drop one or more folders (or files, which bring their folder) onto the module.
  • Hold Shift while dropping to add to the current map instead of replacing it.
  • The right-click menu has: Load folder (replace), Add folder, Remove folder (when several are loaded), Re-scan folders, Include subfolders, and Even out sample levels (normalise).
  • The map's header strip shows analysis progress, then the folder name and sample count. Folders are saved with the patch.

The panel

The map

Click or drag on the map to move the nearest voice's point. Hovering over a dot shows the sample's name. Each voice's ring shows its SPREAD, and the last sample it played is circled and flashes on a trigger.

Global controls

ControlWhat it does
AXISAUTO arranges the map by overall similarity of the sounds. FIXED puts brightness on X and length on Y. The map eases between the two layouts.
LOADChoose a folder (see above).
DRIFT + CVMakes all four points wander slowly. CV: ±5 V = ±50%.
GRAVITYPulls a point towards each sample it plays.
MIX outSum of all voices that don't have their own output patched.

Per voice (four of each, colour-coded)

ControlWhat it does
TRIGTrigger input (1 V threshold). Plays a sample. A new trigger fades the previous sound out over 5 ms rather than cutting it.
X, YThe voice's point on the map. Knobs plus CV; ±5 V covers the whole map. Starting points differ per voice.
SPREADSize of the ring around the point (default 25%).
CHANCE + CVChance that a sample inside the ring plays instead of the nearest one (default 0%). CV: ±5 V = ±50%.
OUTThe voice's own output. Patching it takes the voice out of MIX.

Output level: samples are scaled so a full-scale sample reaches 5 V.

Using it

  1. Drop a folder of one-shots (drums, hits, textures) onto Stella and wait for analysis to finish.
  2. Patch a clock or sequencer gate into a voice's TRIG, and take MIX or the voice's OUT to your mixer.
  3. Move the voice's point on the map to choose a region of similar-sounding samples.
  4. Raise CHANCE and SPREAD to let neighbouring samples sneak in. Use AXIS FIXED when you want X to mean brightness and Y length.

Patch ideas

  • Four-part kit. Place the four voices in different corners of a drum folder, one per trigger lane, each with its own OUT to its own mixer channel.
  • Wandering hats. One voice on a hat region with CHANCE up and a little DRIFT, triggered by a fast clock.
  • Sequenced map. A sequencer or random source into X and Y CV so each hit lands on a different part of the map.
  • Gravity. Raise GRAVITY so a voice gradually migrates across the map as it plays; DRIFT's spring pulls it back towards its knob position.
