Krka does what a quad complex envelope generator like the XAOC Zadar does: four channels that each play a drawn shape as an envelope, an LFO or a one-off gesture, chained together in 16 ways. On the hardware, most of this sits behind menus. Here every parameter has its own knob. The 260 shapes are our own library.

Each row (A to D)

TRIG / FIRETrigger or gate in, and a manual button
BANK, SHAPEPick one of 260 shapes: bank A-Z, shape 0-9 (sheet: [docs/shapes- sheet.png](docs/shapes-sheet.png))
REVPlay the shape backwards
TIME0.85 ms to 30 min. With CLOCK patched it picks note values from 1/32 to 16 bars
RESPBends the level axis (fatter or thinner)
WARPBends the time axis (early or late)
LEVELOutput level (0-10 V at full)
GATELit: while TRIG is high, the shape stops at its peak and waits; it finishes after the gate is released
ANALOGLit: a new trigger carries on from the level it is at instead of jumping back to the start
PHASEIn a chain: start when the channel before reaches this far through its cycle (100 % = when it ends)
REPEATPlay the shape this many more times; fully right = forever (an LFO)
DEST, DEPTH, CVThe CV input goes to one parameter: 1 SHAPE (1 V = one bank), 2 REV (flips above a threshold), 3 TIME (1 V/oct, + = longer), 4 LEVEL, 5 RESP, 6 WARP, 7 PHASE, 8 REPEAT. DEPTH is an attenuverter
OUT, ENDThe envelope, and a trigger when the run ends (every cycle with REPEAT forever)

Quick start. Patch a clock or gate into A TRIG and A OUT to a VCA or filter CV. Turn BANK to B (Percussive) and SHAPE to 3. TIME sets the length. Turn BANK to L for a bouncing ball, or to W for a burst of waves. Turn REPEAT fully right and it becomes an LFO.

The shape library

There are 26 banks of 10 shapes. A attack-decay, B percussive, C plateau (ADSR), D ramps up, E ramps down, F one-cycle LFOs, G gates, H stairs up, I stairs down, J double hits, K multi hits and rolls, L bouncing ball, M swells, N plucks, O resonators (for filter pings), P tremolo envelopes, Q stutters, R random walks, S smooth random, T sample and hold, U spikes, V chirps, W waves, X S- curves, Y humps, Z breaths. The random banks are fixed, so a patch sounds the same every time you load it.

Chains

ALGO decides which channel starts which. A channel started by an earlier one ignores its own TRIG. The first channel of a chain or loop still listens to its TRIG. PHASE on the follower says when it starts: at 100 % it starts when the channel before it ends, at 50 % half way through, and at 0 % at the same moment. With REPEAT, the follower starts during the last repeat. With REPEAT forever, it starts on every cycle. A loop restarts itself, so one trigger sets it running until RESET.

Patches to try

  1. Detroit pad swell. Patch a CLOCK from Live (via the disting NT or VCV's MIDI-CV) and set C to bank M (Swells) with TIME at 2 BARS. Turn GATE on and feed C TRIG with the chord gate from the Hapax. C OUT goes to the pad's filter cutoff and B OUT to its VCA, with B on bank C (plateau). Play with C WARP so the swell opens late.
  2. Self-mutating loop (Autechre direction). Set ALGO 12 (LOOP A>B>C>D) and PHASE about 60 % on all four, so they overlap. Patch D OUT into A CV with DEST 1 SHAPE and DEPTH at 30 %, and C OUT into B CV with DEST 6 WARP. Each lap of the loop changes the next lap's shapes, so the pattern keeps rewriting itself without repeating exactly. Use the four OUTs on filter, FM depth, pan and a delay time. Take the END jacks as triggers for drums.
  3. Bouncing hats and rolls. Set A to bank L (Bouncing ball) or K (Rolls), with TIME at 1/4 synced. A OUT goes to the hat VCA and A END triggers a clap. Set ALGO 2 (A>B) and B to a K shape with PHASE 50 %. This gives a roll that answers half way through.
  4. Arrangement mover. Set CLOCK and give D bank D (Rise) with TIME 16 BARS and REPEAT at 0. Fire D at the start of a section (from Long Form's section triggers) and send D OUT to a filter or Mixxa send. It's a 16-bar build that resets itself. Put a slow A>B chain on a second Krka, driven by D END, for the breakdown.

Notes

OUT is 0 to 10 V. TIME CV is 1 V/oct, and higher voltage gives a longer time. With the clock patched, 1 V moves about two note values. The CV on REV flips the direction when the CV times DEPTH goes over 0.5 V. Right-click to set clock pulses per beat, to let single channels ignore the clock, and to clear presets. MIT licence.
