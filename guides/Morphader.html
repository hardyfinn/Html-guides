Morphader

Stereo audio crossfader for VCV Rack with fourteen ways to get from A to B. Version 2.0.0, MIT licence.

The idea

An ordinary crossfader only changes the level of two signals. Morphader changes how A turns into B. FADE moves from A to B, MODE picks the method, and CHARACTER shapes it. Every mode follows one rule: FADE fully at A is exactly A, FADE fully at B is exactly B, and the middle is where the mode does its work.

Screen

The top line shows the mode number and name on the left and a readout of what CHARACTER is doing on the right. Below it is an A ---o--- B bar showing where the fade really is (after glide), and the glide time at the bottom ("no glide", "glide 250 ms" or "glide 3.2 s").

A few details from the module's own guide:

  • Filter swap: the two filters meet at about 700 Hz in the middle.
  • Gate chop: slow rates give stutter edits. Above about 100 Hz it becomes a timbre, like a pulse-width mix.
  • Tape stop: CHARACTER sets a drop of 1 to 4 octaves.
  • Dub throw: echo time runs from 60 ms to 1 s. Reverb wash: decay runs from 0.6 to 12 s.
  • Grain shuffle: grains run from 5 to 400 ms. Near the middle the grains also jump back in time.
  • Freeze: the moment FADE leaves A, A freezes into a loop of 30 ms to 1 s.

GLIDE and GO

FADE moves at a steady speed, so even a sudden CV jump becomes a smooth sweep. With GLIDE at zero there is only a tiny 2 ms smoothing. The glide range is 10 ms to 10 s for the full trip.

GO (button or trigger) flips which side FADE points at. With FADE at A, press GO and it travels to B over the glide time. Press again and it travels back. The flipped state is saved with the patch. The right-click menu has a GO flipped option that sets it directly.

Patch ideas

  1. Planned section changes. Put the current loop in A and the next section in B. Send a clock divider into GO so it fires every 16 or 32 bars. Set GLIDE to 2 to 8 s and pick a mode for each change: Tape stop for drops, Reverb wash for breakdowns, Spectral for builds. For variety, patch a stepped random CV into MODE, or a slow LFO into GLIDE CV.
  2. Self-mutating texture. Put two related voices in A and B. Send a slow smooth random (about 0.1 Hz) into FADE CV and another into CHARACTER CV, with Grain shuffle or Vocoder selected. The patch keeps drifting between states.
  3. A sound source. Gate chop with CHARACTER above 100 Hz, and two oscillators a fifth apart in A and B. FADE then sets a pulse-width-like blend, so an envelope on FADE gives plucky timbre changes. Ring mode with FADE at the centre works as a ring modulator with a dry/wet control.
