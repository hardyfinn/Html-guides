The idea

These go past noise, crackle and a filter. Each module models a real stage a record goes through, and they are laid out in the order a record travels: Studio (before the lathe) → Vinyl Countdown (cutting, pressing, playing) → Listening Room (after the preamp). On Air plays a record over FM instead, and Scratch is a scratch sequencer.

House rule: every module works on live audio and adds at most 100 ms of latency. Studio, Listening Room and On Air also have CV A/B, BYPASS and presets, like Vinyl Countdown.

Vinyl Countdown (36 HP)

Eight stages in record order, each with an on/off button:

StageControls named in the docs
LATHEhot cut DRIVE, ELLIPTIC (mono bass), HF LIMIT, groove ECHO
RIAA cut curve—
PRESSINGGENERATION, WEAR
GROOVERADIUS, TRACK, SIDE, and PLAY, which sweeps a whole side

PLATTERoff-centre OFFSET, RPM (33 or 45), BEATS
RIAA play curve—
PREAMPVALVE drive, STEP-UP transformer, mains HUM (50/60 Hz)
WRECKdeck B drifts and gets nudged; B is the dry input unless patched
  • RADIUS drives both the inner-groove loss and the wow. DRIVE is the cut level for lathe and groove.
  • BYPASS (top right) passes the dry input straight through, with no delay. Randomize leaves it alone.
  • Presets: the arrows either side of the screen at the top step through 35 starting points, wrapping round (or right-click > Presets). A * after the name means you changed something since loading.
  • Latency: 0, plus 6 ms with PLATTER on, plus up to 100 ms on deck B in WRECK.

Studio (20 HP)

The record before the lathe: CONSOLE (DRIVE, IRON transformer, XTALK) → TAPE master (SAT, BUMP, FLUTTER, AZIMUTH, 7.5/15/30 ips) with DOLBY A around it → GLUE (vari-mu AMOUNT, TIME).

  • Dolby ERROR means it is played back off level. The status reads PATH OK, ENC (never decoded) or DEC (never encoded).
  • Latency 0, +3 ms with TAPE on.

Listening Room (20 HP)

After the preamp: AMP (VOLUME, VALVE/SOLID/CHEAP, LOUDNESS) → SPEAKER (8 boxes from phone to club stack, BREAKUP) → ROOM (SIZE, DAMP, bass MODES, WALL = next door) → EARS (DISTANCE, head ANGLE, PARTY = the morning after). The direct sound is never delayed.

On Air (24 HP)

A record played on FM radio, simulated end to end.

  1. STATION: PROCESSING, ERA (70s/80s/90s), EU/US pre-emphasis and final clipper.
  2. Stereo multiplex with a 19 kHz pilot, then FM at about 768 kHz.
  3. AIR: SIGNAL (carrier to noise), MULTIPATH, MOTION (picket fencing), plus a NEXT station with its own input, LEVEL, and 100/200 kHz spacing.
  4. RADIO: TUNE, and HIFI/CAR/CHEAP sets with stereo blend, high cut and soft mute; MONO.

Latency is under 1 ms. Right-click Eco mode halves the radio sample rate for less CPU.

Scratch (24 HP)

A scratch sequencer that plays like a person.

  • 12 gestures: baby, chirp, forward, transformer, 1- and 2-click flare, crab, orbit, tear, scribble, stab, drag. They run on 8 clocked steps or from a TRIG input.
  • HUMAN adds drifting timing (early and late), speed and stroke changes. SLOPPY makes the fader work messier, scaled by how complex the gesture is.
  • Sources: LIVE (rolling 8 s), GRAB (freeze recent audio) or a WAV (right-click, or drag a file onto the module). Cues snap to hits.
  • Live audio passes straight through between gestures. Choose INSERT or OVER (with DUCK), set the fader CURVE, and use the hamster option. Outputs: HAND, FADER, END.

Patch ideas

These are suggestions built from the chain order above, not presets shipped with the plugin.

  • Full record chain: source → Studio → Vinyl Countdown → Listening Room, so a track is mastered, cut, played and heard in a room.
  • Radio edit: source → Vinyl Countdown → On Air, with SIGNAL lowered and MOTION raised for a drifting broadcast.
  • Second deck: patch a different sound into deck B in WRECK, so it drifts against the main record.
  • Scratch a loop: a drum loop into Scratch on LIVE, a clock into the steps, HUMAN up a little, then out through Vinyl Countdown.
